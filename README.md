image (1024×1024, under 1 MiB): **48 stories across 6 continents**.

## What the image shows

- **Four photo leads:** Russian missiles come down in Poland (NATO calls it
  a grave violation; Warsaw calls it deliberate); Wall Street slides into
  correction as the AI sell-off deepens (Nasdaq −5.3%, Nvidia −14%, and the
  SEC is said to be probing Nvidia's accounts); Kyiv is hit again (Kryvyi
  Rih: 2 dead, 42 hurt; France seeks a UN Security Council meeting); Saudi
  defences intercept Houthi missiles over Riyadh while a tanker is struck
  in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **A world map with 12 numbered markers:** New York, Poland, Kyiv,
  Brussels, Copenhagen, Riyadh, Hormuz, Shenzhen, Gopalpur, Suva,
  Wellington and Sydney.
- **Six region blocks** — Europe, North America, Asia, South America,
  Africa, Oceania — with 28 briefs: the UK–EU deals, Estonia shutting its
  Russia border, the Palisades Fire verdict, the Pete Rose pardon, the
  Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral's thin ending, the India–Japan training pact,
  Argentina 7–0 over Costa Rica, Morocco's AFCON qualification, Fiji's
  drought disaster, and more.
- **A footer ticker:** gold at a 14-month low, F1's V8 engines from 2030,
  Canada's record FIFA rank, the Edmonton Elks' Grey Cup win.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. No network; both runs write identical bytes.
